Each strategy is a fixed script: walk, echo, wait, quiet. **No script reads the listener.** The only thing varied is how long the player waits at the spawn before starting (0 to 20 s in 0.33 s steps, 61 runs), which shifts where the listener is in its patrol. A win rate is therefore "the share of start phases where this plan works", a measure of how much timing luck the plan needs. It is not a measure of people.

Never-sensed floor is the share of the walk spent on floor no echo had revealed before the player stood on it. Closest approach is the nearest the listener got to the player over the run, from ground truth, for evaluation only.

## What each strategy tests

- **Navigator.** Three echoes at the three turns, then straight to the exit. The "1 to 3 pulses suffice" hypothesis.
- **Bait and run.** One echo at the fork pulls the listener toward the fork while you leave by the east corridor.
- **Blind dash.** No echo at all. Needs the route known in advance, so it only exists as a replay or a second attempt.
- **Patient listener.** Walk to the corridor corner, stop for five seconds, then go. Tests whether waiting alone helps without information.
- **Six echoes.** Echo at every chance. The "5 to 6 can remain survivable" hypothesis, and the spam failure.
- **Echo at the junction.** Casting beside the listener corridor. Shows the cost of an echo in the wrong place.
- **Quiet creep.** Quiet step past the listener corridor. Quiet is slower, so the exposure is longer.
- **Echo then stand still.** Echo at the fork and wait there. The mistake the explanation should name.

## Reading the numbers

- Across all 488 runs, suspense captures 123 (25%) and forgiving captures 38 (8%).
- One to three echoes win 100% and 100% of phases in suspense, six echoes win 74%. That fits the prompt's hypothesis that few echoes suffice and many are survivable. It is a hypothesis check on authored scripts, not evidence about players.
- Quiet creep wins only 44% in suspense. At the default footstep reach (2.5 m) sight (4 m) is the real threat, so quiet step is only a slower walk on EC-L01. This is the finding to decide on (EC-D-028).
- "Echo then stand still" never wins (0%), by design: it is the mistake the explanation names.
